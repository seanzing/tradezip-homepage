Why we exist

Great work should not be invisible.

Tradespeople keep homes, neighborhoods and local economies running. Their digital presence should work just as hard as they do.

Right now, nearly 9 million small businesses in America have no website at all. And the trades top that list. Plumbers, electricians, roofers and HVAC technicians. The people who keep every home in the country running are among the least visible online.

The ones who do have a website often built it themselves years ago and have not touched it since. No SEO. No fresh content. No simple way to book a job. That costs them, because 75% of customers judge whether a business is credible based on its website. An outdated site does not just fail to bring in work. It quietly sends work to the competitor down the road.

Meanwhile, that is exactly where customers are looking. 81% of people research a business online before they buy. More than three out of four people who search for a local business on their phone visit a business within 24 hours. When a pipe bursts at 2 a.m., nobody grabs the phone book. They search, and they call whoever shows up looking legitimate.

So great tradespeople lose jobs to average ones with better websites. That is backwards. And it is why we built TradeZIP.
