Questions, answered

Everything you need to know.

Is TradeZIP only for contractors?+

TradeZIP is designed specifically around the way local contractors get discovered, respond to homeowners, quote work and collect payment.

Do I have to buy the complete package?+

No. You can choose a bundle or purchase selected TradeZIP products separately.

Are there any fees beyond the monthly price?+

Yes, and we want to be upfront about it: every plan also includes one $259 annual fee that covers your initial setup and your yearly SEO strategy review. It's the same $259 regardless of which plan you're on. It's billed 14 days after signup — if you don't love what we've built for you in that window, you don't pay it. After that, it renews once a year alongside your subscription. Beyond the monthly plan and this annual fee, there are no other hidden charges — Print the Street campaigns (printing and postage) are only charged if and when you send one.

Does Print the Street include postage?+

Yes. Print the Street is included with every TradeZIP package, or available on its own for $9.99 per month. Campaigns cost $59 for 50 cards, $149 for 150 cards or $349 for 400 cards, including both professional printing and postage.

Can you replace my current website?+

Yes. You'll complete 100% US-based onboarding and work with a real US designer on your new contractor website. We then manage the launch, local service-area pages and ongoing content.

How do website edits work after launch?+

Routine edit requests are submitted through a simple, streamlined process and handled with automation after your site goes live. Your initial onboarding and website design are completed with a real US-based designer.

What kind of custom automations can you build?+

Common projects include lead routing, quote follow-ups, appointment reminders, customer updates, invoice chasing and connecting existing software.
