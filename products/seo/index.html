Questions, answered

Before you get started.

What does Local SEO cost?+

Local SEO is $59 per month plus a $259 annual fee that covers your initial setup and your yearly SEO strategy review. The $259 is billed 14 days after signup — if you don't love what we've built for you in that window, you don't pay it. After that, it renews once a year alongside your subscription. Local SEO is available with a TradeZIP website.

Why do I need a TradeZIP website for this package?+

The local pages and weekly content are built directly into the website. Using the TradeZIP website lets our team manage the structure, publishing and ongoing quality as one system.

Are the 50 local pages all the same?+

No. The pages are written around your services and the locations you serve so they add useful local context rather than simply swapping place names.

Which directories are included?+

We choose the top 10 relevant platforms based on your business type, location and platform eligibility. This can include Google, Apple Maps, Facebook, Foursquare, Yelp and other category-relevant directories.
