Questions, answered

Before you get started.

What does the website cost?+

The Website is $59 per month plus a $259 annual fee that covers your initial setup and your yearly SEO strategy review. The $259 is billed 14 days after signup — if you don't love what we've built for you in that window, you don't pay it. After that, it renews once a year alongside your subscription. There is no contract.

Do I have to build anything myself?+

No. TradeZIP plans, designs and builds the website for you. You provide the business knowledge and feedback; we handle the website work.

Can I speak directly with the designer?+

Yes. You work with a real US-based designer and can share feedback throughout the design process.

What happens to my design if I leave?+

The website design is yours. If you decide to leave, you can take it with you. No questions asked.
