Yes. It qualifies urgency, captures the address and fixture, checks your on-call availability, and either books directly or texts you the details so you can decide on the drive. If you’re available, it books — straight into your calendar.

Will it work with my existing dispatch or CRM?+

Bookings flow into a shared calendar you can see in Google Calendar or your CRM. We’ve integrated with most of the common plumbing tools; if yours isn’t on the list, we’ll wire it up during onboarding.

What about after-hours emergency premium pricing?+

The receptionist quotes the emergency rate before booking so there are no surprises when you arrive. You set the rules once during setup.
